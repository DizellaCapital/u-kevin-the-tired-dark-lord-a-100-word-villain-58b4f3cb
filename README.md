Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb` (pot $2, top 10, FREE). See the bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

The entry is a ~100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain at three in the morning at his war table: he slides Sorrel's wooden horseman back and forth while the plan waits, his robe unwashed since Aldergate, the third cup of tea gone cold by the map, the throne creaking every time he shifts — and the boy king's latest plea folded into a paper bird instead of burned. The tiredness lives in the rhythm and the small deflections, never announced; the speaker stays a villain, with a scheme, spies, and a history.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the monologue to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), validates it (1–600 characters, not only whitespace), and verifies it by read-back.
